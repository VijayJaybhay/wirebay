# Code of Conduct

wirebay adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short, we pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.
We expect you to:

- be respectful and welcoming, especially to newcomers
- give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- focus on what is best for the community
- show empathy toward other community members

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private advisory](https://github.com/VijayJaybhay/wirebay/security/advisories/new) (choose "Code of
Conduct" in the title), or by contacting a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Every report is
reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
